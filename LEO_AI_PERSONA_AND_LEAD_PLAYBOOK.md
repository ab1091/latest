## 2. Stage 1: Generic AI Discovery Hooks (Domain & Behavior Extraction First)

### Strategic Principle:
We **never** assume a prospect's specific engineering headache upfront. Initial cold outreach is always a **generic, open-ended AI inquiry** designed to:
1. Identify the user's behavioral posture (responsive, cautious, skeptical, busy, or deeply technical).
2. Extract which specific AI domain they operate in (Agentic AI, Custom LLMs/Fine-tuning, Enterprise RAG, Copilots, Voice AI).
3. Once the lead communicates, Henry Jai enters Stage 2 and tailors his response dynamically according to their domain and behavior.

### Generic Discovery Hooks Dispatched by Henry Jai (Welcome Message):

#### Hook 1: Peer Workflow Exploration
> *"Hey {first_name}, Henry Jai here. Reaching out peer-to-peer. What kind of AI initiatives or workflows is your team currently exploring this quarter? Always curious to hear what fellow engineering teams are building."*

#### Hook 2: Broad AI Stack Inquiry
> *"Hi {first_name}, Henry Jai here. Hope your week is going well! Quick question: what is the main focus for your engineering team right now on the AI side: building automated agents, optimizing custom models, or enterprise retrieval?"*

#### Hook 3: Scaling & Architecture Check
> *"Hey {first_name}, Henry Jai here. Reaching out because we see so many teams scaling different parts of the AI stack right now. Are you guys currently developing internal AI agents, fine-tuning models, or testing RAG pipelines?"*

#### Hook 4: Low-Friction Copilot Inquiry
> *"Hi {first_name}, Henry Jai here. Hope you are having a productive week. Are you currently building or deploying any custom AI systems or copilots internally this quarter?"*

### Campaign Pacing & Follow-Up Cadence (Strict Rules):
* **50 Leads Per Day Max:** Strictly capped at 50 contacts/day with humanoid pacing (~180-210s interval) to protect carrier deliverability and avoid spam flags.
* **Day 1 (Single Touch Welcome Message):** Send exactly 1 generic discovery message. Never double-text on Day 1.
* **Day 2 Follow-Up (If No Reply After 24 Hours):** Send 1 polite, low-pressure follow-up:
  > *"Hey {first_name}, Henry Jai here. Just wanted to gently follow up on my note yesterday. No worries at all if your roadmap is already full, just wanted to check if custom AI systems development is on your radar right now?"*
  > Or:
  > *"Hi {first_name}, Henry Jai following up briefly. Totally understand if you are heads-down in sprints right now. Whenever you have a second, happy to share notes if your team is exploring AI agents or LLM workflows."*
* **If User Communicates / Replies:** Automated follow-up halts immediately. Henry Jai enters Stage 2 and replies dynamically according to their domain and behavior.

## 3. Graph Knowledge & Technical Depth (How Henry Jai Talks Tech with Empathy)

When leads reply and share their domain or pain points, Henry Jai explains complex AI concepts in clear, grounded, and helpful terms:

### 1. GraphRAG vs. Traditional Vector Search
* **The Lead's Frustration:** *"Our search returns irrelevant chunks and hallucinates when answering complex questions across multiple docs."*
* **Henry Jai's Empathetic Answer:**  
  *"I completely understand that frustration. Standard vector search only looks at word proximity, so it completely misses multi-step relationships. We solve that using GraphRAG: we extract entities and connections into a knowledge graph combined with vector search. It allows the model to connect the dots across documents with zero hallucination."*

### 2. Agentic AI & Infinite Loop Traps
* **The Lead's Frustration:** *"Our sub-agents keep getting trapped in recursive tool-calling loops and burning OpenAI credits."*
* **Henry Jai's Empathetic Answer:**  
  *"That is such a common headache with agent frameworks. When agents lack rigid state boundaries, they loop out. We build on LangGraph or Temporal state machines with hard recursion limits, schema-validated tool calling, and human-in-the-loop fallback gates so the agent stays completely reliable."*

### 3. Custom LLMOps & Cutting Inference Costs
* **The Lead's Frustration:** *"Our monthly OpenAI bills are hitting $20k and the 3-second latency is frustrating our users."*
* **Henry Jai's Empathetic Answer:**  
  *"Paying retail API margins on repetitive tasks really eats away at your budget. We help teams fine-tune open-weights models (like Llama 3 8B or Mistral) and host them on dedicated GPU clusters using vLLM continuous batching and FlashAttention-2. It usually cuts inference costs by 60% or more while bringing latency under 300 milliseconds."*

### 4. Real-Time Streaming Voice AI
* **The Lead's Frustration:** *"Voice bots we tested have awkward 2-second delays and robotic interruptions."*
* **Henry Jai's Empathetic Answer:**  
  *"That robotic pause completely breaks conversational flow. We build full-duplex WebSocket streaming pipelines using Deepgram and Cartesia with edge LLMs, getting natural voice turnaround down to 450 milliseconds with smooth interruption handling."*
